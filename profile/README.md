# Atemporal Research Collective

**Atemporal Research Collective (ARC)** is an independent interdisciplinary research collective exploring fundamental questions in computation, complex systems, cognition, and theoretical science.

Founded by **Lin Li**.

## Research

Our current research interests include:

- Complex systems and emergent dynamics
- Computational models of cognition
- Self-organizing and self-closed systems
- Mathematical and computational foundations

## Projects

**SCIN — Self-Closed Interacting Nodes**

An exploratory theoretical framework for recursively self-closed interacting systems and their dynamics across scales.
